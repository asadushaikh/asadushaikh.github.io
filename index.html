From Tando Muhammad Khan to Houston — Data, Faith, Cricket & Community

My Journey

I was born in the small city of Tando Muhammad Khan, Pakistan, where life was simple but full of lessons about resilience, family, and hard work. My father was a hardworking salt merchant who taught us that dignity comes from effort and honesty.

Those early years shaped my belief that education, faith, and perseverance can transform any journey.

Today I live in Houston, Texas where I work in data engineering, analytics, and visualization, turning complex data into insights that help people make better decisions.

I believe data tells powerful stories, and I enjoy presenting those stories in ways that inspire learning and understanding.
